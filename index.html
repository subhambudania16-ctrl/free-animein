Chapter 1: The Awakening

The entire city was silent.

Thousands of people stood inside the massive Awakening Hall. Today, every sixteen-year-old would discover their Core.

One by one, the young candidates awakened powerful Cores. Cheers erupted whenever someone received a high rank.

Then it was Zero Arin's turn.

The crystal remained completely dark.

Power: 0
Mana: 0
Skill: None
Rank: F

The crowd laughed.

But suddenly, the air inside the hall changed.

A strange black rift appeared behind Zero.

Everyone froze.

Inside Zero's mind, a mysterious voice spoke.

[NULL SYSTEM ACTIVATED]

[User identified: Zero Arin]

[Beginning evolution protocol...]

Zero looked up.

For the first time, he smiled.

They called him the weakest.

They had no idea what they had just awakened.

Chapter 1 — The Boy With Zero Power

“Everyone called him Zero because he had nothing. They were wrong. He wasn't the weakest person in the world. He was the one person the world had forgotten to fear.”

The entire city was silent.

Thousands of people stood inside the Awakening Hall.

At the center of the enormous chamber stood a sixteen-year-old boy.

His black hair fell over his eyes.

His hands trembled slightly.

His name was Zero Arin.

Today was supposed to be the most important day of his life.

The day he would discover his Core.

One by one, the other teenagers placed their hands against the enormous crystal.

“Next!”

A boy stepped forward.

The crystal immediately exploded with blue light.

The examiner's eyes widened.

“B-Rank!”

The crowd erupted.

“B-Rank at sixteen?!”

“That boy will become an elite adventurer!”

The boy smiled proudly.

Zero watched from the back of the line.

Maybe I'll get lucky.

He looked toward the small balcony.

His twelve-year-old sister, Mira, was watching him.

She smiled.

“Good luck, big brother!”

Zero smiled back.

For her.

He clenched his fist.

I'll become strong.

“Zero Arin!”

His name echoed through the hall.

The crowd became strangely quiet.

Zero walked toward the crystal.

Every step felt heavier than the last.

He placed his palm against it.

Nothing happened.

One second passed. Then two. Then five.

The examiner frowned.

“Try again.”

Zero pressed harder.

Still nothing.

The crystal remained completely dark.

Someone laughed.

“Did the crystal break?”

Another voice answered: “No.”

The examiner slowly checked the measurement panel.

His face changed.

“What...?”

The entire hall went silent.

“What is it?” someone asked.

The examiner swallowed.

“Power reading...”

He looked at Zero.

“Zero.”

A few people laughed.

Then the examiner continued.

“Mana: zero.”

The laughter became louder.

“Skill: none.”

Someone shouted: “He literally has ZERO power!”

The entire hall erupted.

Zero stood frozen.

The examiner raised his voice.

“Core classification...”

A red symbol appeared on the crystal.

F-

The weakest possible rank.

Someone shouted: “ZERO IS A ZERO!”

More laughter.

Zero lowered his head.

His fingers curled into fists.

He could hear every word.

“Useless.”

“Pathetic.”

“He'll never survive outside the city.”

“Even goblins could kill him.”

Zero looked toward Mira.

Her smile had disappeared.

But she still raised her fist.

“Don't give up!”

Zero forced a smile.

Then he turned away.

He walked out of the hall.

Nobody followed him.

Nobody cared.

Rain began falling outside.

Zero stood beneath the enormous stone archway.

He looked at his reflection in a puddle.

“Zero...”

He laughed bitterly.

“Even my name is a joke.”

Then—

BOOM!

The ground shook.

People screamed inside the Awakening Hall.

A black crack appeared in the sky.

The examiner ran outside.

His face turned pale.

“A Rift...”

Zero looked up.

The crack grew larger.

[UNKNOWN ENERGY DETECTED.]

Zero froze.

Nobody else reacted.

Because nobody else could see it.

A translucent black window floated before him.

[Sealed Core responding.]

His eyes widened.

“Sealed...?”

The Rift above the city began to expand.

[Emergency condition satisfied.]

[Null System awakening.]

Zero stared at the words.

Then a final message appeared.

[Welcome, Zero Arin.]

[You have been waiting for 16 years.]

Zero's heart began pounding.

“What... are you?”

The system answered.

[I am the power that was never supposed to exist.]

Lightning struck the city.

And for the first time in sixteen years—

Zero smiled.

Not because he had become powerful.

But because, somewhere inside him, something had finally awakened.

End of Chapter 1
