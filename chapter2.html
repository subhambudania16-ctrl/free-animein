ZERO: THE STRONGEST NOBODY

Chapter 2 — The First Quest

The world believed Zero had awakened with nothing. The truth was far more dangerous.

Rain continued to fall over the city.

Zero walked alone through the empty streets.

Nobody congratulated him. Nobody called his name. Nobody cared that something impossible had happened inside the Awakening Hall.

According to the official measurement, Zero Arin possessed absolutely nothing.

[NULL SYSTEM]

User: Zero Arin
Level: 1
Rank: F-
Strength: 1
Agility: 1
Defense: 1
Mana: 0
Skills: None

Zero stared at the floating window.

“So this is my Core?”

The system remained silent.

Zero frowned.

“Then what can you actually do?”

For several seconds, nothing happened.

Then another message appeared.

[HIDDEN CONDITION COMPLETED.]

[NULL SYSTEM IS NOW ACTIVE.]

[BEGINNER QUEST AVAILABLE.]

Zero's eyes widened.

“A quest?”

[QUEST: PROVE YOUR EXISTENCE]

Objective:
Defeat one monster without assistance.

Time Limit: 24 Hours

Reward:
Unknown

Failure Penalty:
Unknown

Zero swallowed.

A monster.

Even an F-Rank monster could kill an ordinary person.

And Zero had just been declared weaker than everyone else.

But strangely...

He smiled.

“For the first time, something is asking me to become stronger.”

The Adventurer Gate

An hour later, Zero stood before the largest Adventurer Gate in the city.

Beyond it were monsters, dungeons and dangerous Rifts.

Normally, only registered adventurers were allowed inside.

Zero placed his hand against the gate.

[WARNING]

User possesses insufficient combat ability.

Entering a dungeon may result in death.

Zero laughed quietly.

“That's exactly why I'm going.”

The gate opened.

Darkness waited on the other side.

Zero stepped forward.

The moment he entered...

The gate slammed shut behind him.

[QUEST STARTED.]

[MONSTER DETECTED.]

[Distance: 37 meters]

Zero stopped walking.

Something growled in the darkness.

Two red eyes appeared.

Then another pair.

And another.

Zero slowly clenched his fists.

Three monsters stepped out of the shadows.

They were goblins.

Their weapons were rusty.

Their smiles were cruel.

Zero looked at them.

Then the system displayed one final message.

[EMERGENCY EVOLUTION CONDITION DISCOVERED.]

[Survive the next 10 minutes.]

Zero's expression changed.

“Ten minutes...?”

The goblins charged.

Zero raised his fists.

And for the first time in his life—

he prepared to fight.

End of Chapter 2
